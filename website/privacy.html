Your media is processed on your headset or PC. No FFSKY account is required. We do not automatically upload your library or viewing history to our servers, and do not include advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs.

1. Media, settings and local data

The apps read the media you select or authorize, including videos, pictures, audio, subtitles, file names, paths/URIs, sizes, durations and thumbnails, to browse, decode, display and process it. Local AI features process media frames on the device running the feature; we do not receive those frames for model training.

Thru3D saves recent media, playback positions, per-file display preferences and thumbnails locally for history, resume and browsing. You can disable saving history and clear existing history. The Windows server saves selected folders, processing settings, generated media and caches locally.

Local diagnostic logs may include device/runtime details, errors, performance information, media paths and network addresses. PC server request logs may include client IP addresses and requested URLs/media. Logs are not automatically sent to us.

2. Headset data and permissions

Thru3D uses head/controller poses, button input and, when enabled and supported, hand joint and gesture data from the headset runtime for viewpoint rendering and interaction. These data are processed during use; we do not build biometric profiles or send them to our servers. Passthrough displays your surroundings through the platform compositor. The app does not capture, save or upload raw passthrough camera images, and does not request microphone recording, eye tracking, contacts or precise location access.

Media and storage: access to videos, images, audio and related files enables browsing and playback. On supported Android versions, “All files access” enables folder browsing across shared storage. A system file picker can also grant access to selected files.

Network, Wi-Fi state and multicast: used to connect to selected sources and discover local DLNA/SMB servers. Internet access supports remote media and optional cloud connections. Head tracking, optional hand tracking and passthrough support immersive playback and controls.

You may deny or revoke applicable permissions in device settings and disable optional features. Functions dependent on that access may then be unavailable.

3. Network sources and cloud accounts

The Windows server makes the folders you configure, media metadata and streams available to clients that can reach its DLNA/HTTP service. Thru3D connects directly to the DLNA, SMB or other media sources you select. These servers receive your network address and browsing/playback requests, plus authentication information where required. Use a trusted network: local DLNA/HTTP traffic may be unencrypted.

When you save an SMB source, Thru3D stores its server address, share path and supplied username locally; saved passwords are encrypted using Android Keystore. Optional 115 and Baidu Netdisk connections process account labels, login session cookies, file lists and playback URLs. Saved cloud sessions are encrypted with Android Keystore on the headset and excluded from Android app backup.

Cloud login is handled by the selected provider. The app sends session credentials and file requests to that provider and connects to its media/CDN servers for playback. The provider receives your IP address and request information under its own policy. This access uses no FFSKY-operated cloud relay. Removing a cloud account from Thru3D does not delete the provider’s account or files.

Software downloads and PC model/runtime downloads contact your selected host, such as GitHub, Hugging Face, a configured mirror or NVIDIA. The host receives ordinary network request information, such as IP address and requested resource. External sites and services apply their own privacy terms.

If you enable LAN WebDAV sharing in Thru3D, devices on your local network with the generated sharing password can browse and stream connected cloud files through your headset. This is optional and uses HTTP without transport encryption. Share the password only with trusted users; turn sharing off to stop access or reset its password to revoke existing credentials.

For other media-server connections, the app also processes server labels/addresses, account IDs, usernames and API keys or access tokens where required. Saved server profiles and credentials are encrypted on the headset using Android Keystore. They are used only to authenticate with and browse/play from the server you select; cover images may be cached locally.

4. Meta Quest, PICO and third-party services

Meta and PICO independently operate their stores, accounts, device services and runtimes. They may process purchase, device and diagnostic data under their own policies. We do not request your Meta/PICO password or import your friends list. Platform-side data processing is separate from the local processing described here.

You can manage platform privacy, diagnostic and account options using the relevant platform’s settings. See the privacy policies of Meta, PICO, and Google / Gmail. For cloud accounts, consult the policy shown by your chosen provider during login.

5. Website and support

This website reads your browser language and stores only your language preference in browser local storage (ptserver-language). Its pages do not embed advertising or analytics trackers. You can clear site data to remove the preference. The web host may process IP addresses, request times, browser/request information and access/security logs to deliver and protect the site; retention depends on the host’s configuration and policy.

If you email ffskyteam@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use these only to respond to your request or provide support. Correspondence is processed through our email provider. Please do not send passwords, session cookies or unnecessary private media.

クラウドのログインは選択した事業者が処理します。アプリはセッション認証情報とファイルリクエストをそのサービスへ送り、再生時にはメディア・CDN サーバーにも接続します。事業者は自身のポリシーに基づき IP アドレスやリクエスト情報を受け取ります。FFSKY 運営のクラウド中継サーバーは使用しません。Thru3D から削除しても、事業者のアカウントやファイルは削除されません。

ソフトウェアや PC 用モデル・実行コンポーネントのダウンロード時には、GitHub、Hugging Face、設定したミラー、NVIDIA など選択した配信元に接続します。配信元は IP アドレスや要求されたリソースなど通常の通信情報を受け取ります。外部サイト・サービスには各自のプライバシー規約が適用されます。

Thru3D のLAN WebDAV 共有を有効にすると、生成された共有パスワードを持つローカルネットワーク上の端末が、ヘッドセットを介して接続済みクラウドのファイルを参照・再生できます。この機能は任意で、転送が暗号化されない HTTP を使用します。パスワードは信頼できる相手にのみ共有し、アクセスを停止するには共有を無効にするか、パスワードを再設定して古い認証情報を無効にしてください。

その他のメディアサーバー接続では、必要に応じてサーバー名・アドレス、アカウント ID、ユーザー名、API キーやアクセストークンも処理します。保存する設定と認証情報はヘッドセットの Android Keystore で暗号化し、選択したサーバーへの認証、参照、再生にのみ使用します。カバー画像は端末内にキャッシュされる場合があります。
